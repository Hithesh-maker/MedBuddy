About MedBuddy

MedBuddy is a comprehensive health tracker app that helps you log daily symptoms, monitor medications, schedule appointments, and gain actionable insights.

Symptom Tracker

Track your symptoms daily and view trends over time. Add notes for each entry and receive insights that help identify triggers, patterns, and improvement areas.

Medication Tracker

Manage prescribed medications with reminders, dosage tracking, and refill notifications. Monitor adherence and ensure timely intake for better treatment outcomes.

Appointment Manager

Schedule and manage medical appointments easily. Receive reminders and log notes from consultations to keep a complete health record.
